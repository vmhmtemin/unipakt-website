

# UniPakt

UniPakt is a web platform designed to connect university students with events, communities, activities, and new social opportunities.

The platform aims to make it easier for students to discover what is happening around their university, participate in activities, and build meaningful connections with other students.

## Overview

University students often have access to numerous events, communities, and activities, but discovering and participating in them can be difficult due to fragmented information and communication channels.

UniPakt brings these experiences together in a single platform.

Through UniPakt, students can:

* Discover university events and activities
* Explore student communities
* Connect with other students
* Participate in social and community experiences
* Track their participation and progress
* Access opportunities based on their engagement

## Platform

The UniPakt website serves as the central platform for discovering and interacting with the UniPakt ecosystem.

The website is designed with a focus on:

* Clear and intuitive navigation
* Modern and responsive interface
* Fast access to events and activities
* Community-oriented user experience
* Scalable architecture for future features

## Engagement System

UniPakt is designed to encourage active participation.

Users can earn points through participation in selected activities and events. These points can contribute to different participation levels and may provide access to exclusive experiences and opportunities.

The system is intended to create a structured way of rewarding active community members.

## Features

### Events

Discover upcoming events and activities organized within the UniPakt ecosystem.

### Communities

Explore communities and student groups based on interests, universities, and activities.

### User Profiles

Create a personal profile and build a record of participation within the platform.

### Participation & Rewards

Engage with events and activities, earn points, and progress through the UniPakt ecosystem.

### Discover

Find new people, communities, activities, and opportunities through a centralized platform.
